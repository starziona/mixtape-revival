# Product steering — Mixtape Revival

## What this is
Mixtape Revival resurrects the dead art of the cassette mixtape as a working
web app. A user picks songs, arranges them on the two sides of a C-90
cassette, writes a dedication, designs the J-card cover, and shares the tape
as a link — or prints the cover on paper. No account, no backend, no
streaming service. The tape is the product.

## Who it is for
- Anyone who grew up making mixtapes (nostalgia, ages 30–60).
- Younger listeners discovering cassettes as a physical, deliberate format.
- People who want to give a playlist as a *gift* — a tape with a message,
  not a link to an algorithm.

## Core value
A mixtape is a message: the songs you chose, in the order you chose them,
on a physical object with a limited length. The app must make that
constraint *felt*: 45 minutes per side, a tape that can run out, a cover you
write on. Convenience features must never remove the tape-ness.

## Non-goals
- We do NOT stream real recordings. There are no copyrighted audio files.
  Playback is generated live with the WebAudio API from small melodies
  written for each catalog song (an original chiptune-style rendition).
- No user accounts, no server, no analytics, no tracking.
- No social feed, no comments, no likes. Sharing is a link to a tape.
- No paid features. Everything works free, forever, offline.

## Tone
Warm, a little dusty, 80s/90s print-shop energy. Playful copy, never
cutesy. The cassette is the hero of every screen.
